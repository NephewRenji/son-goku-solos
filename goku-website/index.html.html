Canonical Feats of Power

Dragon Ball Super Ch. 4 / Ep. 12

Universe-Shattering Fists

During his fight with Beerus in Super Saiyan God, the shockwaves from Goku’s punches rippled across the entirety of Universe 7—a macrocosm containing multiple space-time continuum realms (Hell, Other World, Kaioshin Realm).

"The entire universe will be destroyed if they clash a third time!" — Elder Kai
Dragon Ball Super Ep. 39

Breaking Through Time Distortion

In Super Saiyan Blue Kaioken x10, Goku forced his way through Hit's Tokitobashi (Time-Skip) technique, effectively transcending localized time manipulation through sheer speed and energy output.

Dragon Ball Super Broly Movie

Dimensional Shattering

Clashing against Legendary Super Saiyan Broly, Goku (alongside Gogeta) exerted enough energy to shatter the dimensional fabric of space-time, briefly transporting their fight into a higher dimensional plane.
